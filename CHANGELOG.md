## [1.0.0] — 2026-10-26

- **Initial release.** The keeper: a companion that holds your life
  journal — the events and memories important enough to save —
  through their whole life, from planning to looking back years
  later.
  - **journal-core** — the journal store (`keepsake.json` + a
    generated index) in a `Keepsake` folder in a place you choose;
    the event lifecycle (planning → planned → upcoming → active →
    past → wrap-up → archived, plus postponed and cancelled);
    three-tier visibility (`listed` / `unlisted` / `private`)
    honored in every view and mention.
  - **event-planning** — shaping the days ahead: people, places,
    packing lists, itineraries. Deliberately no RSVP or guest
    management — the journal keeps records, not counterparty state.
  - **capture-and-wrap-up** — moments captured in your words while
    a day happens; a wrap-up conversation afterward with an
    archiving checklist (story, people and tags, media settled,
    copies in more than one place).
  - **look-back** — on-this-day and anniversaries by honest
    date-matching only, plus strict rules for when the keeper may
    mention a memory unasked: listed events only, everyone's
    resurfacing setting respected, quiet dates honored, ask before
    showing, silence is an answer.
  - **people-and-circles** — your people registry, your circles in
    your words, pets included; per-person resurfacing settings
    (`yes` / `less` / `never`); recording a passing changes nothing
    except the settings you choose, and quiet dates are offered,
    never set silently.
  - **The keeper's boundaries** — never invents a memory, never
    deletes, never seals an event against later additions, and
    never records credentials or account details at any tier.
  - Five templates (event story, packing list, itinerary, wrap-up,
    person card) and per-platform install guides with honest
    limits.
