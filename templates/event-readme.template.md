# <Event title>

_This is the event's home in your journal — one per event, kept in the
event's folder. Your keeper fills it with you as the day is planned,
lived, and remembered. Guidance is in italics; delete it as you go, or
leave it — your call._

## When

_Date(s). For a trip, the start and end. If a date is remembered but not
certain, say so here — "mid-October, year unconfirmed" is an honest date._

## Where

_Place name, and the address if you want it kept (as much of it as you're
comfortable writing down)._

## Who was there

_The people, by the names your journal knows them by. Anyone who was
supposed to come but didn't can be noted too — plans that fell through
are part of the story._

## The story

_The day in your own words. This is the heart of the record — what it
was, what happened, how it felt. Your keeper will write what you tell
it, in your phrasing, and won't dress it up._

## Moments

_The small things worth keeping — the quotes, the surprises, the
firsts. (These also live in the event log as they happen; gather the
keepers here.) E.g. "Mateo's first hayride — held on the whole way
out, grinning."_

## Photos & media

_What exists and where it lives: copied into this folder, linked where
it is, or both — with captions in your words._

- _e.g. Group photo by the fire — copied, `photos/` — "everybody, just
  before the s'mores"_

## Links & notes

_Reservations, directions, the recipe someone asked for — links only.
Never passwords, account numbers, or access codes: a journal is kept
for decades and read by family._
