# <Name>

_A readable card for one person in your journal's registry. The
registry record in the store is the source; this card is the copy you
can read at a glance. Your keeper keeps the two in step._

- **Also known as:** _nicknames and name variants — "Nana Rosa"_
- **Circle:** _one of your circles, in your words_
- **Relation:** _in your words — "Alex's dad", "family dog"_
- **Birthday:** _if you keep it; enables birthday anniversaries_
- **Notes:** _what matters about them for the journal — how they take
  their coffee, that they never miss the bonfire, whatever you'd want
  remembered_

## Resurfacing

- **Setting:** _yes / less / never — how this person's memories may
  come back: `yes` may be mentioned, `less` only when you go looking,
  `never` only when you name them directly_
- **Quiet dates:** _dates the keeper never raises their memories
  around, unprompted — or "none"_

## If they have passed

- **Passed:** _date, if and when you want it recorded. Marking it
  changes nothing else — not their circle, not their events, not a
  single setting — except what you choose._
