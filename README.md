# Keepsake Companion Kit

Turn your AI into the keeper of your life journal: the memories and events important enough to save, planned, captured, and kept — so you can look back on them.

It's a portable text-file kit. Install it into the AI tool of your choice (see `INSTALL.md`).

## What it is

Keepsake helps you keep a personal journal of the days that matter —
family gatherings, trips, milestones, the ordinary good days — across
their whole life: planned when they're ahead of you, captured while
they're happening, wrapped up once they're past, and brought back
kindly on anniversaries and quiet mornings years later.

The journal is **yours and lives in your place**: a folder named
`Keepsake`, wherever you choose (a folder on your computer, a synced
drive), holding one folder per event — the story, the moments, the
photos and links — a registry of your people, and a browsable index.
No accounts, no service, no lock-in: plain files your AI reads and
writes on your behalf.

Your keeper is warm and careful with what you entrust to it:

- **It never invents.** A journal that embellishes is fiction — it
  records what you provide, and writes uncertainty down as
  uncertainty.
- **It never deletes and never seals.** Events can be cancelled with
  their reasons kept; archived days can be reopened and added to at
  any time, forever.
- **Your memories come back on your terms.** Every event carries a
  visibility tier — `listed`, `unlisted`, or `private` — and the
  keeper honors it in every list, count, and mention. Memories of
  people who have passed are held with particular care: per-person
  resurfacing settings, quiet dates, and a keeper who asks before it
  shows you a hard day.
- **Some things are never written down.** Credentials, account
  numbers, and access codes are declined, always — a journal is kept
  for decades and read by family.

## What's inside

- **`AGENT.md`** — the keeper: who it is, how it keeps, its
  boundaries, intake, and the governance every companion kit shares.
- **`skills/`** — five modules:
  - `journal-core` — the journal store, the event lifecycle, and the
    visibility tiers.
  - `event-planning` — shaping the days ahead: people, places,
    packing, itineraries.
  - `capture-and-wrap-up` — keeping the day while it happens and
    settling it afterward, with an archiving checklist.
  - `look-back` — on-this-day, anniversaries, browsing and search,
    under strict rules for mentioning the past unasked.
  - `people-and-circles` — your people registry: your circles in
    your words, resurfacing settings, and care around loss.
- **`templates/`** — the event story, packing list, itinerary,
  wrap-up, and person card.

## Getting started

See **`INSTALL.md`** for per-platform guides (Muse AI, ChatGPT,
Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Cursor, and others), each with its honest
limits — including the one that matters most for a journal: whether
your tool can actually save files, and what the keeper does when it
can't.
